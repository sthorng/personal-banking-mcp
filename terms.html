Privacy · Terms

Terms of use

Personal Banking MCP is a private project maintained by the GitHub user sthorng, for personal use with the operator’s own accounts. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Scope

The service is read-only: it lists accounts, retrieves balances and transactions, and calculates cash-flow summaries. It does not initiate payments, move money, open accounts or execute financial transactions. The current implementation uses synthetic data; live integration is not yet enabled.

Bank access

Live access requires the operator’s consent through Enable Banking and the relevant bank. Access is limited to the accounts and permissions authorized by the operator. Enable Banking’s terms and the bank’s terms apply separately. Do not give the service bank passwords or authentication codes.

Limits of the information

Bank availability, consent expiry, provider limits, pending entries and temporary caching can affect completeness and freshness. Summaries depend on the transactions available for the requested period. Transfers are excluded from spending and income only when ownership evidence is sufficient; uncertain transfers may remain included. Currencies are reported separately without conversion.

Check the bank’s own records before relying on a value or making a financial decision. The service’s summaries are informational and are not personalized financial, investment or tax advice.

Operation and privacy

The operator is responsible for securing deployment, restricting access to themselves, protecting provider secrets and reviewing any sharing with an AI assistant. The service may be changed or stopped. Data handling is described in the privacy notice.

Contact

Use the contact options on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. Do not publish private banking information in project discussions or issues.
